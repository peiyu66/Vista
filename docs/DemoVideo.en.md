## Waterfall panorama

[Download Ess-Na-Crub Waterfall — VR360 Mono (MP4, 22.2 MB)](../assets/demo/ess-na-crub-waterfall-vr360-mono-v1.mp4)

A 10-second, 360° monoscopic panorama of a waterfall and woodland, filmed by Bernard Ladenthin. You can turn your head to look around; this is not binocular stereoscopic footage. The video is 2560 × 1280 at approximately 59.94 fps, with H.264 video and two-channel AAC audio. No sign-in is required to download it.

Keep the filename when saving it. Vista can identify **360° / Mono** from the embedded format metadata, with `VR360 Mono` in the filename as a fallback. Previously saved manual format choices take precedence; confirm the format in the controller if needed. Headset viewing comfort has not yet been verified.

The original work, [20180407 132600 Ess-Na-Crub Waterfall](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20180407_132600_Ess-Na-Crub_Waterfall.webm), is by **Bernard Ladenthin**, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This download is transcoded to H.264/AAC, with nominal frame timing regularized and 360°/mono metadata added. The panorama and original audio are retained; no music or narration was added. The footage remains CC BY 4.0, rather than Vista's code license. See the [full credit, source and changes](../assets/demo/ess-na-crub-waterfall-vr360-mono-v1-attribution.txt), and retain that attribution when redistributing it. No endorsement by the creator is implied.

## Color-pattern playback test

[Download Color Patterns — Mono (MP4, 3.6 MB)](../assets/demo/color-patterns-mono-v1.mp4)

This public color-pattern clip was generated for the Vista project with FFmpeg `testsrc2`, for users and App Store reviewers. No sign-in is required to download it. It is an 8-second, silent H.264 video at 1280 × 720 and 24 fps, containing no private footage, people, credentials, or private source information. This is playback input, not a recording of Vista. It does not demonstrate stereo depth, audio, or MKV performance.

## Play a download in Vista

1. Download the video and save it in a folder accessible through Files on Apple Vision Pro.
2. In Vista, select Edit Shortcuts, then Authorize a Local Folder. Select the folder, add its shortcut using Add Shortcut, then select Done.
3. Open the shortcut and select the video. For the waterfall, confirm **360° / Mono**. Keep the Color Patterns filename: `mono` lets Vista select **Mono** automatically on first use, unless a saved manual choice takes precedence. Its projection still defaults to 180°; the pattern is not panoramic or stereoscopic footage and should not be viewed as left-right stereo.
4. Try play/pause and the progress slider. These clips are short; replay as needed.

No Vista account or SMB server is required. This is a separate download, not a built-in video catalog, and it is not automatically added to your folders.
